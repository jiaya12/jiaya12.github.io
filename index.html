About

I am a PhD student at the Department of Information and Computing Sciences, Utrecht University. I work on what happens after a model has been trained. Its scores still have to be turned into decisions, and I study how to calibrate, aggregate and threshold the outputs of a model that cannot be retrained so that those decisions meet stated requirements. My experiments so far use intensive-care data, and the methods are not specific to medicine.

Calibration · uncertainty quantification · decision-making under uncertainty · subgroup reliability · distribution shift

Doctoral Research

Thesis title: Turning Risk Scores into Reliable Decisions Without Retraining the Model. Two completed studies cover staffing under uncertain demand and alerting with a sensitivity guarantee for each patient group. A third study asks whether such decision rules keep their guarantees when the data change over time. Supervised by Ad Feelders and Arno Siebes.
